Reading Grammar

A Day at the Park

1. Read the passage

Last Sunday, Mina went to the park with her family. The weather was sunny, so they decided to have a picnic. Mina brought some sandwiches, and her brother made lemonade. While they were eating, a small dog ran toward them. Mina was surprised, but the dog was friendly. They played with the dog for a while and returned home in the evening. Mina had a wonderful day.

💡 색상이 칠해진 단어를 누르면 문법 설명을 볼 수 있습니다.

2. Learn the grammar

Grammar Point: Simple Past vs. Past Continuous

Grammar Form Example
단순과거 동사의 과거형 Mina went to the park.
과거진행 was/were + 동사-ing They were eating.
